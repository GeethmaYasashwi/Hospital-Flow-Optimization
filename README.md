# Hospital-Flow-Optimization
## Hospital Patient Flow Optimization Network

**Agent Team:**

- **ED Admission Predictor**: Forecasts emergency department volume 6-12 hours ahead
- **Bed Allocator**: Assigns patients to beds considering department capacity, isolation needs, staff ratios
- **Discharge Coordinator**: Identifies patients ready for discharge, coordinates with home health/rehab
- **OR Scheduler**: Optimizes operating room schedules considering surgeon availability, urgency, equipment
- **Staffing Optimizer**: Adjusts nurse assignments based on patient acuity and predicted admissions
- **Transport Coordinator**: Manages patient transport, lab specimens, equipment movement
